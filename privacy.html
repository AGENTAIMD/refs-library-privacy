Refs Library Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who uses this app

Refs Library is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not distributed to anyone else and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Refs Library requests read-only access to the owner's own Pinterest account: the account profile, the owner's boards (including secret boards the owner chooses to sync) and the Pins on them. It cannot create, edit or delete Pins or boards, and it does not access other people's accounts.

How the data is used

The app downloads the images of the owner's saved Pins, together with their titles, descriptions and links, into a private folder on the owner's computer. The owner uses this folder as a personal reference library for their own design work (colour palettes, layouts, typography and motion ideas). Saved images are used for inspiration only and are not republished. Short summaries, such as the number of new Pins synced, may be shown to the owner by their own local assistant software.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Pinterest access and refresh tokens are kept only in the owner's password manager and in memory while a sync runs. Downloaded images and descriptions stay on the owner's computer. The owner can delete the library folder at any time; items removed from Pinterest can be removed from the library on the next sync.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under the list of connected apps, which immediately stops any further syncing.
